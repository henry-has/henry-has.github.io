Trace Daily

Draw copper traces on a circuit board.
  1. Goal: connect the battery to every chip. Each chip needs at least the target voltage to run.
  2. Draw: drag across the board to lay copper. Pick a thickness (1 2 3). Drag over an existing trace to change its thickness. Right-click or drag to erase (touch: Erase button).
  3. Why thickness matters: every bit of trace wastes some voltage (more current, longer or thinner means more lost). Thick traces lose less.
  4. But copper costs: thickness is the price. Stay under the copper budget and use as little as you can for more stars.
  5. Hot traces: a trace carrying too much current for its thickness glows red and would burn out. Chips downstream share a trunk, so a trunk carries all of their current added together.
  6. Holes can't be drawn over. Route around them.
