Spotweld

A battery packaging puzzle. Weld cylindrical cells into a pack so current flows through it easily and evenly. Lower mΩ is better.

  1. Split the cells into S groups of P cells. 10S4P means 10 groups of 4. Cells in a group are in parallel; groups are in series, S1 → S2 → S3 …
  2. Left-click or drag to add cells to the highlighted group. When it's full you move to the next. Click a group in the series chain to go back to it.
  3. Right-click or drag to erase. On a phone, tap a cell of the highlighted group to remove it (drag to remove more). Ctrl Z undoes.
  4. Touching cells get a strip. Each group must touch the one before it. More strips between groups is better; hot strips glow.
  5. ★ finish · ★★ within 5% of par · ★★★ beat par.
How batteries work
  • Series (S) stacks groups end to end to add voltage. Every group carries the full pack current.
  • Parallel (P) puts cells side by side in a group to share that current.
  • Current passes from one group to the next through nickel strips where cells touch. More strips between two groups means less resistance and less heat.
  • The cell that carries the most current runs hottest and wears out first, so you want every cell in a group doing an equal share.
