How it works

S is how many groups are wired in series (sets voltage). P is how many cells are in each group, side by side (sets capacity and current). 7S3P = 7 groups of 3 cells.

Fill group S1, then S2, and so on. Each group should be one touching cluster and must touch the group before it, so current can flow from PACK − to PACK +.

Touching cells are joined by nickel strip: 2 mΩ per cell spacing. Each cell is 15 mΩ and carries about 15 A.

Score = half pack resistance (vs a perfect busbar) + half current sharing (how evenly cells in a group carry load), multiplied by the share of groups that are one piece.

Groups alternate orientation, so strips are on both faces. Use Flip to see the other side.
