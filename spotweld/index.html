Spotweld

Lay out a cylindrical-cell battery pack with low, even interconnect resistance.
  1. Goal: split the cells into S groups of P cells (10S4P = 10 groups of 4). Cells in a group are in parallel; groups are wired in series in number order, 1 → 2 → 3 …
  2. Place cells: left-click or drag over cells. When a group is full you move on to the next one. Click a group chip, or start a drag on one of its cells, to work on it again.
  3. Erase: right-click or drag (touch: Erase mode). Ctrl Z undoes.
  4. Interconnects: wherever a cell of group n touches a cell of group n+1, a strip joins them. Odd links (1–2, 3–4 …) sit on Side A, even links on Side B. More strips between two groups means a stronger link.
  5. Stars: ★ finish · ★★ within 5% of par · ★★★ beat par (on First Weld, match it).
